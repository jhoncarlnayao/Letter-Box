About Letterbox

Letterbox.

A letter, sealed & sent.

Letterbox is a tiny tool for writing a private letter and sending it as a sealed envelope — no accounts, no servers, just a link or a picture.

1

Write — fill in who it's to, your message, and who it's from.

2

Preview — hover or tap the envelope to watch it open.

3

Send — copy a share link, or download it as an image, already opened.

Everything renders locally in your browser — nothing you type here is sent anywhere.
